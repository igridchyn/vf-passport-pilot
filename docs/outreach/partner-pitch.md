# partner-pitch.md — Channel-partner pitch and co-run confirmation (zh-CN + EN), draft

Date: 2026-10-06. **Status: draft**, for the founder's review on the FD.4 comms card. Task
FD.4 (`docs/planning/validation-plan.md`); used by the founder at CH.2. Serves test **C** (a
Chinese-speaking channel partner agrees in writing to co-run the outreach) and test **D**
(the partner's introductions are a channel of PREREG-F §5.1).

**The loop sends nothing.** The founder chooses whom to approach (card M-001), finds the right
person, and sends these texts by hand (hard rule 1). Names and contacts stay in the founder's
own files (hard rule 2). This file names no organisation: `[organisation]` and `[role title]`
are filled in per approach from `channels.md` §2.

**What the texts follow.**
- `channels.md` §1, the terms every approach must state: introduce only; no seller data to the
  partner; pay never depends on the outcome; no post-gate promise; honest framing; overlap
  said up front.
- PREREG-F §5.1 (no outcome-dependent pay; results per partner and without the top partner)
  and §6.2 (the partner never receives form data, contacts or LOIs).
- The offer, price and in-development wording of the page (FD.2), as in `kit.md`.
- **Do not mention Responsible-Person brokerage** to a tier-1 agent or a tier-2 vendor that
  sells EU-rep services: it would compete with them (`channels.md` §5). The pitch says only
  that RP services are not included.
- **Do not offer money.** The test has no commission and no per-result pay. If a partner asks
  to be paid, the only form allowed is a fixed fee for a bounded scope, which is a cost card
  for the founder (hard rule 6; `channels.md` §6).
- **The free GPSR explainer** is offered only if the founder decides to give one, and then on
  `channels.md` §1's conditions: content only, every regulatory statement with a primary
  source, `/claims-audit` and a comms card before it is given, and any paid webinar tool on a
  cost card.
- **Copy check by an agent that sells EU-rep services:** show it only the kit's texts
  (`kit.md`), not the landing page, which says PassportPilot plans to introduce RP providers
  (`channels.md` §5). Or accept that it sees the page, and say so on the card.

Every zh-CN line needs a native speaker's check and is marked [zh-check]. The partner itself
can check the Chinese of the whole kit (`channels.md` §6; PREREG-F §5.4's free option).

> **Subject:** An invitation: co-run a 4-week seller-demand test of a GPSR documentation tool
> in development (no payment is taken during the test, and there is no commission)
>
> Dear [role title],
>
> I am the founder of PassportPilot, an independent project based in the EU. 〔This message
> was written with a translation tool; replies in Chinese are welcome.〕
>
> PassportPilot is in development. It is not available yet, so it cannot be bought, and no
> payment is taken now; its launch date is not fixed, and whether it launches has not been
> decided; a seller who signs up gets early access when it launches. It is planned to draft,
> for each product, from questions a seller answers in
> Chinese: a GPSR product risk analysis, a GPSR technical documentation file, safety warnings
> and instructions in the languages of the EU countries where the seller sells, and
> product-safety text to paste into marketplace listings. The seller reviews, corrects and
> attests to the drafts. It does not certify or test products, give legal advice, act as EU
> Responsible Person or sign Declarations of Conformity; EU Responsible Person services are
> not included.
>
> Before building it, I want to run an honest 4-week test: do sellers of textiles and
> apparel, furniture or jewellery to EU consumers want it at the planned first-year launch
> price of €249 per year for up to 10 products? Sellers can sign up for early access and can
> sign a non-binding letter of intent; no payment is taken from anyone during the test.
>
> **What I am asking of [organisation]:**
> 1. Introduce the test 1:1 to suitable sellers among your clients and contacts, and share a
>    sign-up link that carries your own code. Please do not introduce sellers in a group chat,
>    and do not introduce your own staff or relatives.
> 2. Adapt my Chinese texts to your own voice. Please send me each adapted text for approval
>    before you use it; the price, the scope and the "in development" wording stay as they
>    are.
> 3. Answer "what is this?" in Chinese, using the short answers I provide; pass any other
>    question (for example fines, deadlines, legal questions, Responsible Person, or price
>    questions the short answers do not cover) to me. 〔If you are willing, also check my Chinese copy.〕
> 4. Tell me during the test how many sellers you introduced (a number, no names).
>
> **What stays with me, not with you:** sellers fill in the sign-up form or write to me
> directly. You do not receive or collect their answers, contacts or letters of intent, and I
> will not ask you for any seller data. Your own conversations with your clients remain your
> business.
>
> **What I can offer during the test:** an early look at a tool your clients may ask about; a
> say in what it covers (product categories, languages, export format); and the aggregate
> result at the end, which shows whether this kind of seller wants it. 〔I can also give your
> clients a free GPSR explainer session (content only, no product pitch).〕
>
> **What I cannot offer:** there is no commission and no payment per sign-up or per letter of
> intent, and no promise of any cooperation after the test. Any commercial discussion waits
> until the test has been decided. In my own evaluation, results are counted per partner (as
> numbers only) and also recomputed without the partner that contributed the most, so the
> result does not hang on one network.
>
> 〔I understand that [organisation] already offers GPSR documentation. PassportPilot would
> overlap with that, and I would rather say so up front.〕
>
> If you are interested, I will send a one-page summary of how the co-run works, and you can
> confirm by e-mail or WeChat. Planned start: [date], for 4 weeks; you can stop at any time.
> PassportPilot is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any marketplace or public authority.
>
> Thank you!
