GPSR product-safety documents for your EU listings: answer in Chinese, review the drafts

PassportPilot is a tool in development for sellers who ship textiles and apparel, furniture or jewellery to consumers in the EU. You answer questions about your product in Chinese. It drafts the product-safety documents in the languages of the EU countries where you sell. You review, correct and attest to every draft.

Who it is for

  • You sell on an EU marketplace, or ship from your own online store to consumers in the EU.
  • At least one of your products is in textiles and apparel, furniture, or jewellery.

What it is planned to draft for each product

  1. A GPSR product risk analysis.
  2. A GPSR technical documentation file.
  3. Safety warnings and instructions in the languages of the EU countries where you sell.
  4. Ready-to-paste product-safety text for your marketplace listings: the manufacturer, Responsible Person and safety-information fields.

Why these documents

The EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR, Regulation (EU) 2023/988) has applied since 13 December 2024. Major EU marketplaces ask sellers for product-safety information in their listings, such as the manufacturer, the EU Responsible Person and safety warnings.

PassportPilot is planned to draft the documents behind that information: the risk analysis, the technical file, and the warnings and instructions in the language of each country where you sell.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (EUR-Lex). This is general information, not legal advice.

How it is planned to work

  1. You answer questions about your product, in Chinese.
  2. PassportPilot drafts the documents and the listing text.
  3. You review, correct and attest to them. You stay responsible for your product and its documents.

What PassportPilot is not

  • It does not certify products, and it does not test them.
  • It does not give legal advice.
  • It does not act as your EU Responsible Person, and it does not sign Declarations of Conformity.

Under GPSR, a product it covers may be placed on the EU market only if an economic operator established in the EU is responsible for it (Art. 16(1)). This GPSR rule does not apply to products subject to specific requirements of EU harmonisation legislation (Art. 2(1)); for some of those, such as toys and electrical equipment, Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 sets the same requirement (Art. 4). Check which rules apply to your product. If you need such a Responsible Person, PassportPilot plans to introduce you to providers of that service, who set their own prices.
