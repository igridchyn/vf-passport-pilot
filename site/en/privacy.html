Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the information you give when you sign up for early access to PassportPilot, a product in development. Version of [publication date].

1. Who is responsible

The controller is [operator name and postal address, as in the imprint], the founder developing PassportPilot. Contact for anything about your data: contact@example.com.

2. What we collect

On the sign-up form

  • Your answers on where you sell, what you sell, what kind of business it is, and your role in it.
  • Your shop name and main marketplace (required, so that each business is counted once).
  • Your answers about your catalogue size, your current compliance provider, what you pay for it, your view of the planned price, and whether you would pay the first year in advance at launch.
  • Only if you write it: anything you add in the optional free-text question on what would stop you using a tool like this.
  • The time you submitted the form.
  • The code of the link you came from (for example, which channel shared it). It is part of the link; there is no cookie.
  • Only if you choose: one business contact (WeChat ID, e-mail or phone) and your consent to be contacted.
  • Only if you sign the letter of intent: your business name, your role, and your e-signature, which may show your name.

We do not ask for your name as a separate field, ID numbers, store log-ins or addresses.

In direct conversations

If we write to each other on WeChat or by e-mail, whoever writes first, we receive what you write and any letter of intent you send.

On this website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The hosting provider processes technical connection data, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages.
