**The new L5 paragraph** (section "What PassportPilot is not", `site/en/index.html` and
`site/index.html`; the Chinese is marked `zh-check` for the native-speaker check):

> Under GPSR, a product it covers may be placed on the EU market only if an economic operator
> established in the EU is responsible for it (Art. 16(1)). This GPSR rule does not apply to
> products subject to specific requirements of EU harmonisation legislation (Art. 2(1)); for
> some of those, such as toys and electrical equipment, Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 sets the same
> requirement (Art. 4). Check which rules apply to your product. If you need such a
> Responsible Person, PassportPilot plans to introduce you to providers of that service, who
> set their own prices.

> 根据 GPSR，该法规适用的产品，只有在有一个在欧盟境内设立的经营者对其负责时，才能投放欧盟市场（第 16 条第 1 款）。受欧盟协调立法具体要求约束的产品，不适用 GPSR 的这一条规定（第 2 条第 1 款）；其中部分产品（例如玩具和电气设备）由法规 (EU) 2019/1020 规定了同样的要求（第 4 条）。请核实适用于您产品的规定。如果您需要这样的欧盟负责人，PassportPilot 计划为您介绍提供该服务的机构，价格由这些机构自行确定。

**Two things to know.**
1. **Where the text came from.** The EUR-Lex web page came back empty to the loop's fetch tool,
   so it read the same Official Journal PDF from the EU Publications Office (which runs
   EUR-Lex). That is the authentic text of the original act; no consolidated version was read
   and amendments were not checked on EUR-Lex. If you want the EUR-Lex page itself, open
   https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj in a browser and look at Art. 2(1), 16(1),
   22(9) and 52 (about 5 minutes).
2. **An open question for the product, not the page.** EU harmonisation legislation includes
   REACH and the textile-labelling regulation (2019/1020 Annex I). If textiles or jewellery
   count as "subject to specific requirements" of those, GPSR's manufacturer duties (including
   the technical file, Art. 9) and Art. 16 would not apply to them. The Commission's guidance
   on this was not read. The page no longer depends on the answer; the product plan does. It is
   logged for E0.1 (decision log #19). PREREG-F §1.1 (approved) still carries the old L5
   sentence as a description of the page; nothing in its bars or numbers changes, so the loop
   left it alone. Say in your note if you want it amended (that is a new card).
