**What was found.** While desk-checking the build spec's open items (task MV.1,
`docs/research/2_spec_open_items.md` §4.1), the loop found a competitor that the refresh (R1)
missed: **Sevarto** (Germany). It sells *generated* GPSR drafts. The first is the safety
information an EU listing must show (GPSR Art. 19), with warnings **in German**. The second
is a risk-analysis PDF per product. The seller approves each draft, and delivery is "within
minutes". Prices are **one-off per order**: **€99 net for up to 10 products**, €199 net for
up to 30 and €399 net for up to 100. The source is the vendor's own pricing page, read as a
search snippet on 5 Oct 2026. No Chinese interface and no output in other EU languages was
seen.

**Why it is yours.** Card D-002 asks you to fix the test price (PREREG-F option A: €249/yr for
up to 10 products). Sevarto's offer has the same shape as ours: drafts the seller reviews and
attests. Its 10-product price is lower, although a one-off order and a yearly price do not
compare directly. For comparison, option A already sits below EUProof Pro (€390/yr for 10
products) and above EUProof's 1-product plan (€190/yr), both from R1 §3.

**Options**
- **keep**: no change. D-002 stays as drafted (recommended option A), and Sevarto is noted as
  a competitor. Consequence: the test measures willingness to pay €249/yr while cheaper
  German-language generators exist.
- **reconsider**: before answering D-002, look again at options B (€199/yr for 5 products) or
  C (€290/yr for 25). You could also change the €150–300 price band itself; that is card
  D-001's "adjust" answer, and it means editing `decisive-test.md`, which only you may do.
  Consequence: the price stays open until you answer D-002.

**Recommendation: keep.** As with EUProof (R1 §3), feature parity is not the wedge. These
are: Chinese input, output in *several* EU languages, and the China channel. None of them
was seen at Sevarto. One more vendor does not move the decisive test's band. The test also
records sellers' reasons for saying no, which is where a cheaper rival would show up.

risk. Worth keeping in mind before FD.3.
