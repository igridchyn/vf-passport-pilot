**What is asked.** Put the landing page and the sign-up form online, and check that sellers in
mainland China can open both. This is the demand test's front door (test D,
`docs/planning/decisive-test.md`). It is yours because it spends money (a domain, about
US$10), needs your identity on the imprint and privacy notice, and needs accounts and a
mainland phone only you can arrange (hard rules 1, 2, 6). The loop deploys nothing, buys
nothing and contacts nobody. About 90 minutes of your time, plus waiting for the Chinese
copy check. **You need a computer with a terminal and a text editor** (steps 1 to 6); the
rest works from a phone. Reading this on a phone now and doing it later is fine.

**Wait for these first (do not publish before all four):**
1. **M-002** (page, form copy, LOI, privacy notice) approved, your edits applied.
2. **D-001** (refresh: continue / adjust / stop) answered. The page and the form say
   "€249 per year for up to 10 products"; a different band changes the copy first.
3. **EUR-Lex check**: done by the loop (FD.3a, decision log #19): L1 and the marketplace
   statement confirmed, L5 reworded. This gate is met once you approve card **M-005**.
4. **M-007** (the WeChat consent tick T3 on the form and the consent paragraph in the kit,
   which follow from the section 5 choice D+B, decision log #21) approved. Step 4 builds the

**Steps.**
1. **Work in a copy, never in the repo.** The imprint and privacy notice need your name,
   postal address and an e-mail address. The repo must hold none of them (hard rule 2; the
   acceptance check also rejects e-mail addresses). Take the copy from the checkout that has
   M-002's edits (merge the loop's branch `autorun/loop` into `main` first), and re-copy if
   the repo changes later, or you may deploy stale files. In a terminal, from the repo's
   top folder: `bash scripts/make-deploy-copy.sh ~/passportpilot-deploy`. It refuses a folder
   inside the repo or one that already has files, copies `site/`, removes the internal review
   comments, and lists every placeholder left with file and line. Edit only that copy. (If
   you already have the Tally link, add `--form-url <link>` and skip step 5.)
2. **Choose and buy the domain first** (Cloudflare Registrar, a `.com`): a plain name with no
   marketplace or authority name in it, checked for trademark clashes. The page address
   `https://<your domain>` (no trailing slash) is needed in steps 3 and 4. You can leave
   hosting for step 8.
3. **Fill the placeholders** in the copy. The script's output from step 1 lists them; to
   list them again: `grep -rn 'class="placeholder"\|{{\|example.com' ~/passportpilot-deploy`
   (`{{FORM_URL}}` hits in `index.html` and `en/index.html` are expected unless you used
   `--form-url`; step 5 fills them.) Edit by hand:
   - `imprint.html`: operator name and postal address; what the law of the country where you
     are established requires (e.g. VAT ID if you have one; Germany: § 5 DDG); the contact
     address (1 place).
   - `privacy.html` (zh) and `en/privacy.html`: publication date (use the day you publish);
     controller (same as the imprint); contact address (2 places in each); hosting provider
     (Cloudflare, or Netlify if you use it); your e-mail provider; the supervisory
     authority of your country; and the safeguard paragraph in section 5 (transfers
     outside the EEA). **That paragraph is a legal judgement the loop cannot make.** Draft
     paragraphs to choose from, zh-CN and EN, each with its GDPR article and what it commits
     you to: `docs/outreach/privacy-safeguard-options.md` (paragraph H for hosting and e-mail,
     plus options D and B for WeChat: the choice recorded in decision log #21, which you
     confirm by answering this card; the two paragraphs go one after the other, D first).
     Or pay for a legal review: answer `hold`, note
     "legal review: quote asked", and the loop opens a cost card. A different choice: say so
     when you answer this card, and the loop redrafts T3 and the consent paragraph.
   - Use a role address you control for the contact, not a personal one, if you can.
   - After filling, turn `<span class="placeholder">X</span>` into plain `X`.
   In total: 3 places in `imprint.html`, 8 in `privacy.html`, 8 in `en/privacy.html`; the
   contact address is 5 of them.
4. **Build the form in Tally** (free account; stay on the free plan) by pasting the blocks
   of `docs/outreach/tally-paste.md` in order: its section A is the setup checklist, section C
   the text (the same as `form.md` and `loi.md`), zh-CN first and English under it, the fixed
   status line on every screen, nothing pre-ticked. There is **no branching on
   qualification** (everyone can finish); only L2 to L4 (the letter-of-intent fields) appear
   after L1 is ticked.
   - Add a **hidden field named `ch`** (Tally help: "hidden fields").
   - Replace `{{PAGE_URL}}` (the page address from step 2, no trailing slash, it is followed
     by `/privacy.html`) and `{{CONTACT}}` (the role address) in the form text. In the LOI on
     screen 5 (`docs/outreach/loi.md`), `[page address]` is the same page address and
     `[page date]` is the publication date from step 3. Do all this in Tally only.
   - Turn off every integration, notification to third parties and analytics. Accept Tally's
     data-processing terms (PREREG-F §6.4). The form is not embedded in the page; the page
     only links to it.
   - D3's yen edges: ¥[B1] and ¥[B2] are €150 and €300 at the ECB reference rate on the day
     you publish, rounded to ¥100; `tally-paste.md` section B has the ECB link, the rounding
     rule and a worked example. Write the rate and its date in your answer.
   - Test: open `<form link>?ch=TEST`, submit one test answer, check the `ch` column says
     `TEST`, then delete the test submission.
5. **Put the form link into the page:**
   `cd ~/passportpilot-deploy && sed -i 's#{{FORM_URL}}#<your Tally form link>#g' index.html en/index.html`
   The page's button then carries `?ch=web`.
6. **Check what you changed.** Run the `diff -r` command the script printed at the end of
   step 1. It compares your copy with a fresh one, so only your fills and the form link show.
   Then open `index.html` in your browser: both languages, the privacy and imprint links, the
   phone-width view.
7. **Native-speaker check of the Chinese.** Give a reviewer: the printed-to-PDF pages from
   step 6 (`index.html`, `privacy.html`, `imprint.html`), and a plain document into which you
   paste only the zh-CN lines of the form (screens 1 to 6) and of the LOI: section D of
   `tally-paste.md` has them ready. Leave out the build notes and the English; they are not for the reviewer. Every
   `zh-check` mark in the files is something to check, including the WeChat menu label in
   the note under the sign-up button (“···” then “在浏览器打开”).
   - **Who:** a trusted Chinese speaker, unpaid. A channel partner from **M-004** only if it
     does **not** sell EU Responsible-Person services (M-004 step 3 excludes #1, #2, #3,
     #6, #8): the landing page describes introducing such providers, and M-004 only promises
     that a partner can check §2, kit §2b and §6. Asking it for more is a favour you ask
     explicitly. A paid reviewer is a cost: answer `hold`, note "review: quote asked".
   - Apply the reviewer's corrections in `~/passportpilot-deploy` and in the Tally text. List
     in your answer which lines changed, so the loop can bring the repo copy in line
     (a `card:A-002` item does it).
8. **Hosting (Cloudflare, recommended):** Cloudflare dashboard, Workers & Pages: create a
   **Pages** project by **direct upload** of `~/passportpilot-deploy` (a plain folder; no
   build command; if the dashboard now routes this under Workers static assets, it is the
   same thing). **Do not connect the repo**: it is private and holds the plan documents.
   Attach the domain from step 2 under the project's custom domains. **Leave Web Analytics
   off** (no trackers, CLAUDE.md style rule). Do not share the project's default
   `*.pages.dev` address: it is reported blocked in China (PREREG-F §5.2). Fallback if the
   custom domain does not load in China: Netlify with a custom domain, same folder, free tier.
9. **Check it from mainland China.** A friend or relative with a mainland phone, or a
   partner you have told this is a test and asked not to forward. A partner's introduction
   of a seller starts the 4-week window (M-004), so this must not be one. Give the tester
   the link and nothing else, **before** any seller or outreach message sees it. From a
   **WeChat chat** and from a normal browser:
   - the page opens and is readable (note roughly how many seconds);
   - `/privacy.html`, `/imprint.html` and the English page open;
   - the form link opens with `?ch=TEST` (never a P[n] or `web` link) and one test
     submission goes through; delete it afterwards;
   - the note under the sign-up button is right: does the “···” menu in that WeChat
     version really say “在浏览器打开”? If not, say the real label in your answer;

     browser: every message must then carry the link as copyable text plus a QR code

   If the page loads but the form does not: PREREG-F §5.3 fallback. 1:1 route first (WeChat
